# Dataset provenance

`synthetic_training.csv` is generated by `ml-service/preprocessing.py` with NumPy random seed 42. It contains 1,344 trips across 48 synthetic vehicles, 28 trips each. It is artificial development data and contains no personal or measured vehicle data. No external dataset or licence is misrepresented.

Target: `mileage` in km/L. Group: `vehicle_id` (not a predictor).

Features: `vehicle_type` (car/suv/motorbike/van/truck), `fuel_type` (petrol/diesel/hybrid), `engine_l`, `weight_kg`, `distance_km`, `avg_speed` in km/h, `idle_minutes`, `traffic` (light/moderate/heavy), `road` (city/highway/mixed).

The generator computes a consumption rate from engine, weight, speed deviation and categorical adjustments, adds an idling component and noise, then converts to km/L. These relationships are assumptions, not experimentally validated physics. Every synthetic training score must be labelled accordingly.

For your own dataset, supply these columns and an honest --source description. Include stable vehicle identifiers for grouped testing. Do not include target-derived historical mileage from the test period as a feature. Cleaning coerces numeric fields, drops duplicates/invalid targets and impossible negative measurements, and removes unsupported categorical values. Remaining missing predictor values are imputed within the training pipeline. At least 100 cleaned rows and 5 vehicles are required. Do not load untrusted joblib model files.
